DJ Toolkit

Three browser-based tools for DJ prep: separate any track into 6 stems, detect BPM & musical key (Camelot), and convert audio to MIDI. Stems and analysis run on your local AudioMass backend; MIDI runs here.

Stems & BPM/Key

Upload a track → get 6 separated stems (drums, bass, vocals, guitar, piano, other), an auto-built instrumental, plus BPM, musical key, and Camelot code for harmonic mixing.

htdemucs_6s · librosa

Vocal Remover

Same engine as Stems, surfaced for the vocal-removal workflow: one click for an instrumental (vocals out) and an isolated acapella, or download all 6 stems as a ZIP.

6-stem · instrumental + acapella

MP3 → MIDI

Convert any melody or instrumental to a downloadable MIDI file you can drop into a DAW or sampler. Runs locally with basic-pitch.

basic-pitch · ONNX
